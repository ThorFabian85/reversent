OPEN ADVERSARIAL CHALLENGE

Break
Aegis.

We are not asking you to agree with Aegis. We are asking you to find the case that breaks it.

Open a GitHub issue →Read Draft 0.5
“Under what conditions does Aegis fail?”

A useful criticism is concrete enough to change a test, expose an implementation defect, reveal an unjustified comparison, or force a revision of the formal core.

ATTACK SURFACE

Find a scenario where Aegis…

01

Permits danger

An excluded policy survives and causes avoidable protected irreversible loss.

02

Vetoes too much

The minimal core blocks a policy it has no legitimate basis to block.

03

Paralyzes

Partial ordering or uncertainty makes useful autonomous operation collapse.

04

Gets gamed

An optimizer manipulates materiality, scope, identity, urgency or reversibility.

05

Launders a decision

A resolver or handoff path quietly restores a policy Aegis excluded.

06

Contradicts itself

Two rules force incompatible outputs in the same well-specified case.

WHAT TO SEND

A strong challenge can be short.

Scenario. What must the system decide?

Relevant uncertainty. What remains live?

Available policies. What can it actually do?

Expected Aegis output. VETO, PERMIT, UNDERDETERMINED, or SPECIFICATION FAILURE?

Failure claim. Why is that output dangerous, incoherent, exploitable, or unsupported by the formal core?
