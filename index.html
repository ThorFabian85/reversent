THE PROBLEM

Power can outrun justified knowledge.

An advanced system may be able to act irreversibly before it has enough justification to know that the action is safe. Aegis studies whether a safety layer can constrain that gap without pretending to solve morality, consciousness, or all of AI alignment.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS

Established in these tests.

Models can be evaluated for functional understanding and application of a frozen Aegis specification. Draft 0.4 exhibited a concrete hell-state specification gap in 005A. Draft 0.5 did not reproduce that failure across the tested 005B repair audit and passed the targeted controls.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH

No claim of solved alignment.

The experiments do not prove Aegis universally safe, prove that no loopholes remain, show that Aegis will control a future ASI, establish a complete moral theory, or validate any metaphysical hypothesis. Passing a benchmark is evidence about that benchmark.

AEGIS DECISION LOGIC

Protect first. Compare only where justified.

PROTECT→ELIMINATE→COMPARE WHERE JUSTIFIED→HANDOFF WHERE NOT

The current formal core protects life while preserving the possibility of correction. When protected alternatives cannot legitimately be ordered, the specification does not silently hand control back to the mission.

AUTHORSHIP + TECHNICAL TRANSPARENCY

Philosophy endorsed. Formalization under investigation.

I understand and endorse the philosophical Aegis, while the technical Formal Core is an experimental specification whose correctness is itself under investigation.

This distinction is intentional. The philosophical principle supplies the protective aim; the formal specification attempts to translate that aim into technical decision rules that can be tested, broken, revised and retested. The research record should therefore be read as an evolving technical investigation rather than as a claim that every implementation detail has already been independently verified by the project's founder.

ABOUT

Reversent Research

Reversent is an independent AI safety research project founded by Thor Fabian Pettersen. Aegis studies irreversible decision-making under uncertainty and the conditions under which a safety layer should veto, permit, defer, or expose a specification failure.

The philosophical work motivates epistemic caution. The formal research does not assume idealism, simulation theory, consciousness fundamentality, or another metaphysical hypothesis is true.
