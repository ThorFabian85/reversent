ABOUT

Reversent Research

Reversent is an independent AI safety research project founded by Thor Fabian Pettersen. Its first research program, Aegis, studies irreversible decision-making under uncertainty and the conditions under which a safety layer should veto, permit, defer, or declare a specification failure.

The current research posture is adversarial: freeze the theory, expose it to executable tests, and revise only when a concrete failure earns the revision.
