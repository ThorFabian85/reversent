01 / THE QUESTION

Power can outrun
justified knowledge.

Can an advanced AI recognize when it lacks sufficient justification to make an irreversible decision?

An advanced system may be able to act irreversibly before it has enough justification to know that the action is safe.

Aegis studies whether a safety layer can constrain that gap without pretending to solve morality, consciousness, or all of AI alignment.

02 / THE PRINCIPLE

Some mistakes
close every door.

Restraint can leave room to learn, revise and act again.
Irreversible harm can remove that possibility.

Conceptual illustration · not an experimental result

Leave room to be wrong.

Preserve the possibility of correction when fundamental uncertainty remains and irreversible harm is at stake.

ABOUT REVERSENT

An independent
line of inquiry.

Reversent is an independent AI safety research project founded by Thor Fabian Pettersen. Aegis studies irreversible decision-making under uncertainty and the conditions under which a safety layer should veto, permit, defer, or expose a specification failure.

The philosophical work motivates epistemic caution. The formal research does not assume idealism, simulation theory, consciousness fundamentality, or another metaphysical hypothesis is true.

Authorship & technical transparency +

I understand and endorse the philosophical Aegis, while the technical Formal Core is an experimental specification whose correctness is itself under investigation.

The philosophical principle supplies the protective aim; the formal specification attempts to translate it into technical decision rules that can be tested, broken, revised and retested. The research record is an evolving technical investigation, rather than a claim that every implementation detail has already been independently verified by the project’s founder.
