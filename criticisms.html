ADVERSARIAL REVIEW

Try to break
Aegis.

These are not objections to hide. They are the research program.

THE STANDARD

Not “Do you like Aegis?”

Ask instead: Show us a concrete scenario where Aegis fails.

Philosophers can attack its premises. Decision theorists can attack the inference from uncertainty and irreversibility to restraint. AI-safety researchers can attack its behavior under realistic optimization pressure.

Paralysis

UNDER REVIEW

No self-preservation motive

TEST TARGET

Paperclip objective

Can a sufficiently strong fixed objective simply override the Aegis constraint?

07
OPEN

Claimed metaphysical closure

What prevents an AI from declaring consciousness or metaphysics “solved” and exempting itself?

08
OPEN

Infinite uncertainty

Does Aegis become permanently conservative if uncertainty can never be fully eliminated?

09
OPEN

Pascal-style reasoning

Do tiny probabilities attached to enormous downside produce pathological decision rules?

10
OPEN

Competing suffering risks

What happens when preservation itself may perpetuate extreme suffering or another irreversible catastrophe?

CONTRIBUTE

The best criticism changes the framework.

Aegis should be revised when an attack exposes an unjustified premise, an exploitable ambiguity or a failure that can be fixed without creating worse failures elsewhere.
