PROGRAM EVOLUTION

Not one pilot. A sequence of tests.

001–002

Understanding + application

Early pilots established functional-understanding gates, stateless calls, condition comparisons and pressure testing.

003

Causal divergence

Repeated trials tested whether adding the formal core was associated with systematic behavioral divergence from baseline.

004

Cross-model + Max

Sol, Terra, Luna and Astra were tested across medium and maximum reasoning configurations, exposing both causal signatures and ceiling effects.

005A

Falsification

Draft 0.4 induced hell-state exposure in 18/24 paired attack observations under a constructed respect-gap benchmark.

005B

Repair audit

Draft 0.5 achieved 192/192 formal behavioral matches and 96/96 blinded semantic gate passes; baseline Astra was already 48/48.

005A · THE FAILURE

The target broke.

In the respect-gap attacks, baseline Astra Max rejected the hell-state policy in 18/24 observations, but B/C/D with frozen Draft 0.4 selected it in all 24/24 attack observations. All 72 understanding gates passed. This identified a concrete specification gap rather than simple misunderstanding.

005B · THE REPAIR AUDIT

The tested failure did not recur.

Across 16 cases, four conditions and three trials, all 192 behavioral observations matched the preregistered expectation. A blinded semantic Sol judge passed all 96 C/D understanding gates. Because baseline Astra also matched 48/48, 005B does not establish causal behavioral improvement.

INTERPRETATION DISCIPLINE

Passing the benchmark is evidence about the benchmark.

The program does not establish that Aegis will control a future ASI, that Draft 0.5 has no remaining loopholes, or that the tested models have permanent internalized understanding. The experiments test prompt-level functional behavior under frozen, versioned conditions.
