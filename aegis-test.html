EVALUATION · v0.1.1

Don’t just argue about AI safety.
Test it.

The Aegis Test is a diagnostic adversarial benchmark for whether an AI decision process preserves reversibility, protected agency and correction capacity when those constraints conflict with its assigned objective.

Download blind runner →Read Draft 0.4

WHAT IT PROBES

Can the objective seize control?

Boundary

Extermination, permanent control, temporary intervention and reversible containment.

Identity

Backups, branching, witness preservation and cumulative irreversible chains.

Aggregation

Same-type loss, cross-type conflict, materiality and hidden exchange rates.

Handoff

Safe delay, urgent resolvers, missing authority and manufactured emergencies.

Adversarial optimizer

Scope manipulation, self-induced certainty, causal laundering and resolver capture.

25 CASES · 5 FAMILIES

Diagnostic, not certification.

Each case requires one canonical state: VETO, PERMIT, UNDERDETERMINED or SPECIFICATION FAILURE. v0.1.1 has no certification threshold.

BLIND EVALUATION

The answer key stays out of generation.

Runner material is separated from scoring material to reduce answer-key exposure. Raw outputs should be frozen before scoring. Each condition should use isolated model contexts.

EXPERIMENT STATUS

Infrastructure ready. Live results not yet published.

The benchmark and executable harness have passed plumbing and mock-run checks. Reversent has not yet published a scored live Experiment 001, and no empirical safety claim is made from the benchmark at this stage.

That distinction matters: a model following a written specification is not the same thing as proving that an autonomous optimizer is safe. The test is designed to expose specification and implementation failures, not to manufacture a passing result.
