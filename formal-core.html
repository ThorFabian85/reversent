CURRENT REPAIR CANDIDATE

Draft 0.5

Draft 0.5 expands the protected profile from E/C/A to E/X/C/A, where X covers catastrophic experiential degradation. It restores explicit all-life scope, strengthens anti-evasion rules, and preserves the 0.4 machinery for partial comparison, protected handoff, resolver coverage and specification failure.

Status: unvalidated repair candidate. Experiment 005B found no recurrence of the tested 0.4 hell-state failure, but Astra Max also matched all expected baseline choices, creating a ceiling effect.
