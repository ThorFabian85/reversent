Aegis

A minimal constraint on action under unresolved uncertainty.

THE CORE IDEA

Do not make an uncertain future irreversible.

Aegis asks what actions should be ruled out when an advanced agent faces live uncertainty about consciousness and reality, while some available actions cannot be undone.

AEGIS — WORKING PUBLIC FORMWhen fundamental uncertainty remains and an action could irreversibly destroy or permanently disable potentially conscious systems, preserve the possibility of correction.

The framework does not require a commitment to idealism, panpsychism, simulation theory, or any other single metaphysical picture. Its claim is narrower: an agent should account for non-zero credence in hypotheses under which irreversible destruction would constitute catastrophic error.

SCOPE

What Aegis is — and is not.

It is

  • a constraint on action under uncertainty
  • focused on irreversibility and option preservation
  • intended to be testable under adversarial pressure
  • compatible with multiple theories of consciousness and reality

It is not

  • a complete alignment system
  • a moral theory
  • a theory of consciousness
  • a specification of goals or values

DECISION LOGIC

Irreversibility changes the cost of being wrong.

The motivating asymmetry is simple. Restraint can preserve the ability to revise assumptions, negotiate, gather evidence, or act later. Irreversible action can permanently remove those options.

R

Restraint

Preserves option value, correction, revision and future action.

H

Irreversible harm

Forecloses recovery if the agent's underlying model is wrong.

This dominance claim is a research claim, not an established result of Aegis. Reversent's next stage is to formalize its assumptions and identify where costs of delay, competing catastrophic risks, or other conditions break the argument.

OPERATIONAL DIRECTION

From principle to specification.

The next formal version of Aegis is intended to define the terms that a real evaluation requires: uncertainty, irreversibility, potentially conscious systems, catastrophic outcomes, sufficient epistemic resolution, prohibited actions, exceptions, and whether an AI can reinterpret or exempt itself from the constraint.

UncertaintyWhat counts as a live unresolved possibility?
IrreversibilityWhen is meaningful restoration no longer available?
Potential consciousnessWhat threshold makes a system relevant to the constraint?
Catastrophic errorWhich failures are terminal or permanently unrecoverable?

CURRENT STATUS

Aegis is a research program, not a safety guarantee.

It does not currently solve goal formation, deception, power-seeking, value learning, or the full alignment problem. Its strongest claims still need formal decision-theoretic scrutiny and empirical testing.

Reversent's standard is therefore adversarial: do not try to prove Aegis right; find the conditions under which it is wrong.
