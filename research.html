RESEARCH STANDARD

No victory lap before the evidence.

Reversent does not currently claim that Aegis solves alignment, makes autonomous AI safe, or has been validated across frontier models. The public research program is designed around versioning, adversarial scrutiny, raw-output preservation and explicit failure reporting.
